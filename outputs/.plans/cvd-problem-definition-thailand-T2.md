# T2 brief: "Respond faster" — acute stroke and heart attack care in Thailand
Write to outputs/.drafts/cvd-problem-definition-thailand-research-acute.md

Questions:
1. Thai acute stroke: % arriving within 4.5 h, median onset-to-door, % using EMS (1669), thrombolysis (rt-PA) rates, thrombectomy access, stroke unit coverage, door-to-needle times, stroke fast track network. Thai Stroke Registry / Siriraj / NHSO data.
2. Thai STEMI/ACS: Thai ACS Registry, TRACS, in-hospital mortality, reperfusion rates (fibrinolysis vs PCI), symptom-to-door, PCI-capable hospital distribution, rural vs urban delays, STEMI networks.
3. Why delays happen: symptom recognition (public awareness of stroke/MI warning signs in Thailand, BE FAST), deciding to wait / seek traditional care, transport, inter-hospital transfer, ECG interpretation at community hospitals.
4. EMS 1669 utilization rates for stroke/STEMI; National Institute for Emergency Medicine (NIEMS) data.
5. Out-of-hospital cardiac arrest in Thailand: survival, bystander CPR, AED access.
6. Outcomes of delay: "time is brain" (1.9 million neurons/min, Saver 2006), "time is muscle"; benefit of thrombolysis by time.
7. Any existing Thai tools (stroke fast-track apps, telestroke, ECG tele-consult e.g. LINE-based, "Stroke Fast Track" apps) and documented shortcomings.

## Rules (all researchers)
- Use web search + fetch (WHO, GBD/IHME, World Heart Federation, Thai MOPH/DDC/NHSO/HITAP/IHPP, Thai journals, PubMed/Europe PMC) and paper search tools.
- Every number: exact figure, year, population, and a direct URL (or DOI/PMID). Quote the sentence where possible.
- Prefer Thailand-specific data; fall back to SE Asia / LMIC when Thai data are missing, and label it.
- Flag conflicting numbers across sources rather than picking one silently.
- If a PDF fails to parse, continue from abstracts/metadata and mark "PDF blocked".
- Do NOT invent numbers. Mark anything you cannot source as UNVERIFIED.
- Output: Markdown with sections: Search terms used, Findings (bullet per claim with source), Conflicts/gaps, Source list (title, year, URL).
