# T3 brief: "Detect earlier / prevent before it begins" — Thailand
Write to outputs/.drafts/cvd-problem-definition-thailand-research-prevention.md

Questions:
1. Hypertension in Thailand: awareness, treatment, control rates (NHES V, NHES VI); undiagnosed proportion; trends; rural vs urban; age/sex differences. Same for diabetes and dyslipidemia.
2. Thai NCD screening: NHSO/UCS screening for adults 35+, verbal screening by village health volunteers (VHVs/อสม.), Thai CV risk score (Thai CV Risk Score, Rama-EGAT), coverage of screening, follow-up after positive screen (loss to follow-up / referral completion).
3. Atrial fibrillation: prevalence in Thai elderly, proportion undiagnosed, anticoagulation use (COOL-AF registry), AF-related stroke share.
4. PAD: prevalence in Thai/Asian high-risk patients (ABI studies), underdiagnosis, diabetic foot amputations in Thailand.
5. Secondary prevention: post-stroke / post-MI medication adherence in Thailand, recurrence, readmission, cardiac rehab uptake (very low?), loss to follow-up after discharge.
6. Workforce: cardiologist/neurologist per population, doctors per population in rural provinces; VHV numbers (~1 million) and their workload/tools.
7. Evidence of benefit if fixed: e.g., 10 mmHg SBP reduction → ~20% fewer major CV events (Ettehad 2016 Lancet); WHO HEARTS; SPRINT; any Thai modelling.
