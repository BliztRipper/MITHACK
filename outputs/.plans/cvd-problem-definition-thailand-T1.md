# T1 brief: CVD burden (global + Thailand), costs, trends
Write to outputs/.drafts/cvd-problem-definition-thailand-research-burden.md

Questions:
1. Global: CVD deaths per year (WHO / GBD 2021-2023 / World Heart Report 2023/2024), prevalence (~half a billion?), share of deaths, share in LMICs, premature deaths.
2. Verify the hackathon claims: "affects over half a billion people", "one-third of Thais", "nearly 20 million deaths/year", "80,000 deaths in Thailand". Find source and exact wording for each; note if they mix definitions.
3. Thailand: annual deaths from IHD, stroke (Thai MOPH / DDC / Strategy and Planning Division), stroke incidence/prevalence, ranking as cause of death/DALYs, trends 2015-2025.
4. Thailand risk factor burden: hypertension prevalence (NHES V 2014, NHES VI 2019-2020), diabetes, obesity, smoking, dyslipidemia. Population ageing (aged society 2021-2023, super-aged ~2031).
5. Economic burden: cost of CVD/NCDs to Thailand (WHO/UNDP NCD investment case for Thailand), catastrophic health expenditure, productivity loss, caregiver burden.
6. PAD global prevalence (GBD/ Song et al. Lancet Glob Health 2019) and any Thai estimates; amputations.

## Rules (all researchers)
- Use web search + fetch (WHO, GBD/IHME, World Heart Federation, Thai MOPH/DDC/NHSO/HITAP/IHPP, Thai journals, PubMed/Europe PMC) and paper search tools.
- Every number: exact figure, year, population, and a direct URL (or DOI/PMID). Quote the sentence where possible.
- Prefer Thailand-specific data; fall back to SE Asia / LMIC when Thai data are missing, and label it.
- Flag conflicting numbers across sources rather than picking one silently.
- If a PDF fails to parse, continue from abstracts/metadata and mark "PDF blocked".
- Do NOT invent numbers. Mark anything you cannot source as UNVERIFIED.
- Output: Markdown with sections: Search terms used, Findings (bullet per claim with source), Conflicts/gaps, Source list (title, year, URL).
