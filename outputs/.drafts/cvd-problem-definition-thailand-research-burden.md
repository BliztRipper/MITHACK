## Findings

### Global
- WHO (fact sheet, CVDs): "An estimated 19.8 million people died from CVDs in 2022, representing approximately 32% of all global deaths. Of these deaths, 85% were due to heart attack and stroke. Over three quarters of CVD deaths take place in low- and middle-income countries. Out of the 18 million premature deaths (under the age of 70) due to noncommunicable diseases in 2021, at least 38% were caused by CVDs." URL: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/cardiovascular-diseases-(cvds)
- WHO fact sheet also: "It is important to detect cardiovascular disease as early as possible so that management with counselling and medicines can begin." Same URL.
- World Heart Federation, World Heart Report 2023 (p.3, executive summary): "More than half a billion people around the world continue to be affected by cardiovascular diseases, which accounted for 20.5 million deaths in 2021 - close to a third of all deaths globally"; "Up to 80% of premature heart attacks and strokes can be prevented"; "Around 4 in every 5 CVD deaths occur in low- and middle-income countries". URL: https://world-heart-federation.org/wp-content/uploads/World-Heart-Report-2023.pdf
- WHO hypertension fact sheet: "An estimated 1.4 billion adults aged 30–79 years worldwide had hypertension in 2024; this represents 33% of the population in this age range... An estimated 600 million adults with hypertension (44%) are unaware... Approximately 320 million adults with hypertension (23%) have it under control." URL: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/hypertension
- WHO stroke fact sheet: "In 2021, stroke was the third leading cause of death and disability globally, with an estimated 93.8 million cases... 11.9 million new cases... 1 in 4 adults predicted to experience a stroke in their lifetime." URL: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/stroke
- PAD: Song et al. 2019 Lancet Glob Health (PMID 31303293): "Globally, a total of 236·62 million people aged 25 years and older were living with peripheral artery disease in 2015, among whom 72·91% were in LMICs. The Western Pacific Region had the most peripheral artery disease cases (74·08 million)." Global prevalence age ≥25: 5.56%. DOI 10.1016/S2214-109X(19)30255-4 (DOI not re-verified; PMID verified).

### Thailand
- World Heart Federation World Heart Observatory, Thailand profile (IHME GBD source, accessed by WHF Feb 2026): "Overall number of deaths from CVD (2024) 120,370; Crude CVD mortality 184/100,000; Age-standardized CVD mortality 103/100,000; Age-standardized CVD incidence 442/100,000; Prevalence of hypertension (women) (2019) 29.2%; (men) 29.1%; Prevalence of tobacco smoking (2019) 21.2%; Action plan for CVDs: Yes." Note: page header says 2024 but source text says "Total number of deaths due to cardiovascular disease in 2021" — internal year inconsistency. URL: https://world-heart-federation.org/world-heart-observatory/countries/thailand/
- Thai DDC (Department of Disease Control) press release, 24 Oct 2023 (Thai): MOPH public health statistics 2023: Thailand had 349,126 stroke patients and 36,214 stroke deaths; more men than women; "most deaths were under 70 years old" (ผู้เสียชีวิตส่วนใหญ่มีอายุน้อยกว่า 70 ปี); "90% of strokes are preventable". URL: https://ddc.moph.go.th/brc/news.php?news=37914&deptcode=brc
- Thai review (TCI ThaiJo, "Heart Disease and Stroke: Global Burden and Lessons from Thailand"): "In Thailand, more than 65,000 people die from heart disease each year, and there were approximately 350,000 new stroke cases reported in 2023". URL: https://www.tci-thaijo.org/en/articles/health04-3603 (secondary source; primary not traced).
- Stroke epidemiology in Thailand (Suwanwela, J Stroke 2014, PMID 24741559): stroke prevalence 1.88% among adults ≥45; mean age of onset 65; stroke mortality still rising over prior 5 years.
- Thai national ischemic stroke cohort (Ann Med 2026, PMID 42504160): 888,003 IS patients, 18-year follow-up; overall mortality 47.6%: in-hospital 6.4%; ≤30 days post-discharge 8.2%; >30 days post-discharge 33.1%. "highlight the need for integrated acute and post-discharge stroke care".
- Thai national stroke claims 2017–2020 (BMJ Open 2023, PMID 37280024): 386,484 incident stroke patients; mean age 65; mean annual cost 37,179 THB/patient; only 32% received rehabilitation; 4-year survival 66.5%; treatment outside Bangkok associated with higher mortality.

### Thai risk factors (NHES)
- Hypertension (NHES 2004–2019/20; BMC Public Health 2024, PMID 39538168): 2019–2020 age-standardized prevalence 25.7%. Among hypertensives: 51.5% aware, 47.9% treated, 22.7% controlled. Control rose 8.8% (2004) → 30% (2014) then fell to 22.7% (2019–20). Awareness 30.7% → 55.8% (2004–2014) → 51.5% (2019–20). "Despite universal health coverage, hypertension awareness has not improved and blood pressure control has decreased in the past five years."
- Diabetes (Sci Rep 2025, PMID 40866600): age/sex-standardized prevalence 7.5% (2004) → 10.1% (2020); only 62.8% aware, 42.9% treated, 20.5% controlled (2020).
- Rural HTN cohort (Glob Heart 2025, PMID 41487451): among 1,000 rural treated/identified hypertensives, control rate 63.9%; LDL ≥100 in 58.7%; BMI ≥25 in 51.5%. (Different denominator than NHES: people already engaged in care.)
