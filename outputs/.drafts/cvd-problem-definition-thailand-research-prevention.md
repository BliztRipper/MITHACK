## Findings

### Silent risk: hypertension and diabetes (the biggest modifiable driver)
- NHES 2019–20 (PMID 39538168): HTN prevalence 25.7%; of hypertensives 51.5% aware, 47.9% treated, 22.7% controlled. Control fell from 30% (2014). Under UHC "every citizen has access to primary care including free hypertension treatment" yet awareness stagnated and control fell.
- NHES 2004 (J Hypertens 2008, PMID 18192831): prevalence 22.0%; prehypertension 32.8%.
- NHES III (Asia Pac J Public Health 2012, PMID 20685665): 30% of hypertensives diagnosed, 24% treated, 9% controlled (historical baseline).
- Diabetes 2020 (PMID 40866600): prevalence 10.1%; 62.8% aware; 42.9% treated; 20.5% controlled.
- 2024 Thai hypertension guideline (Asian Biomed 2025, PMID 41728364): keeps 140/90 diagnostic cut-point, introduces "BP at risk" 130–139/80–89, advocates home BP monitoring or ABPM to confirm diagnosis.
- Rural cohort (PMID 41487451): 10-yr CV risk >20% in 7.9% of hypertensives (12.7% males); LDL ≥100 mg/dL in 58.7%.
- Physician-led community model, rural Thailand, preprint (Research Square 2026, DOI 10.21203/rs.3.rs-8568478/v1): attributes high control to accurate BP measurement, physician reconfirmation, "acceptance of asymptomatic hypertension", free management. Preprint — not peer reviewed.
- Factors associated with adherence in hypertension + pre-DM, semi-rural Thailand (Patient Prefer Adherence 2026, PMID 42246022): knowledge, social support, patient–provider relationship, and convenience of travelling to services correlated with adherence.

### Atrial fibrillation
- COOL-AF Thailand registry (n=3,405 NVAF, 27 hospitals; Thromb Haemost 2024, PMID 38626898): 17.9% had bleeding events; survivors of major bleeding had aHR 4.44 for death, 4.49 for stroke/SE; "Up to 30% of patients who survived major bleeding..." (truncated; likely OAC discontinuation — not verified).
- NOAC knowledge among Thai AF patients (Healthcare 2026, PMID 42793518): mean age 71.9; only 34.4% knew missed-dose management; age ≥75 independently associated with low knowledge (aOR 2.11).
- 2025 Thai AF guideline exists (PMID 41311874).
- Thai population prevalence of undiagnosed AF: NOT FOUND in this run.

### Peripheral artery disease (limbs)
- Lower-northern Thailand primary care, 884 T2DM patients (Diab Vasc Dis Res 2020, PMID 33158366): PAD (ABI ≤0.9) prevalence 7.2%.
- PAD-SEARCH, 6,625 Asian T2DM ≥50 yrs incl. Thailand (Diabetes Res Clin Pract 2007, PMID 16950543): PAD prevalence 17.7%.
- Thai T2DM + PAD cohort (PLoS One 2025, PMID 40378162): 7-year cumulative all-cause mortality 36% (6.4/100 person-years); critical limb ischaemia HR 5.26 for all-cause death vs asymptomatic PAD. → asymptomatic PAD is detectable only by measuring (ABI); symptomatic CLI is late.

### Post-event (secondary prevention)
- Only 32% of incident stroke patients received rehab (PMID 37280024).
- 8.2% die within 30 days of discharge after IS (PMID 42504160).
- Thai cardiac rehabilitation uptake: no title-level hits (data gap).

### Community workforce
- Village health volunteers (VHVs, อสม.) prioritise physical-health tasks due to policy; organisational constraints and workload noted (PLoS One 2025, PMID 40146743, mental-health focus).
- VHVs used a LINE "Stroke Sign by Sticker" application, which improved stroke knowledge, n=60 (J Educ Health Promot 2024, PMID 39297115).
- Telehealth platform for community health leaders doing NCD risk assessment in rural northern Thailand, feasibility n=120 (SAGE Open Med 2026, PMID 42405357): acceptability 92–97%.
- Number of VHVs (~1 million commonly cited): NOT VERIFIED in this run.

### If fixed (benefit evidence)
- Ettehad 2016 Lancet meta-analysis (123 trials, 613,815 participants; PMID 26724178): every 10 mmHg SBP reduction → RR 0.80 for major CVD events (95% CI 0.77–0.83); CHD RR 0.83.
- COBRA-BPS (Jafar, NEJM 2020; PMID 32074419): community health worker-led multicomponent intervention in rural Bangladesh, Pakistan, Sri Lanka; SBP fell 9.0 vs 3.9 mmHg at 24 months (difference 5.2 mmHg).
- WHF World Heart Report 2023: up to 80% of premature heart attacks and strokes preventable.
- DDC 2023: "90% of strokes are preventable" (WSO messaging).

## Conflicts / gaps
- HTN control: national survey 22.7% (all hypertensives in population) vs rural clinic cohort 63.9% (patients already in care). Gap is mainly upstream: not diagnosed/not treated.
- AF and PAD population-level Thai data are thin; this is itself a finding (under-measured).
